# Business Brief: Fees (Crown Heights Academy)

### The Business
Crown Heights Academy is an independent primary and secondary school (JSS1 to SS3) in Lagos, Nigeria, serving 450 students. Fees is the school’s dedicated web application, providing each registered family with direct, authenticated access to their private tuition ledger, payment receipts, and approved fee policies.

### The User
The primary user is a parent or guardian, typically aged 28 to 52. They access the application on low-cost Android smartphones (primarily Tecno, Infinix, or Samsung Galaxy A-series) running mobile Chrome. They purchase internet data in small prepaid bundles (100 MB to 1 GB), encounter frequent power cuts, and rely on erratic 3G or congested 4G connections. They are non-technical and need clear English in short, direct sentences without financial jargon.

### The One Thing That Must Work
Exact, private retrieval of the student's fee balance, line items, and confirmed receipts, tied to the bursar’s daily ledger stamp. A parent must see only their own child’s records—the database strictly isolates rows by student ID. If the bursar has not reviewed the ledger, the balance states "Not loaded yet"; it never shows zero or guesses.

### Three Questions Only This Business Can Answer
1. **"What is my exact outstanding balance for this term, and what specific items make up that total?"** (Tuition, development levy, science lab, PTA, optional bus or lunch).
2. **"Did the school confirm my recent payment, and can I produce a valid receipt to show at the school gate?"** (Verified against Flutterwave or the bursar’s physical receipt book).
3. **"What are the school's fee deadlines and policies, and which specific staff member can resolve an issue if my bill looks wrong?"** (Answered strictly from approved school records, naming Mrs. Bello, the bursar, or the school owner).

### Interface Tone and Typography Drivers
School fees involve high anxiety. An unconfirmed balance can mean a child is turned away at the gate on Monday morning. The interface must not feel like a commercial marketplace, a fintech game, or a flashy SaaS tool. It must feel calm, sober, trustworthy, and authoritative—like a crisp official school bill or paper passbook. This demands high-contrast, text-only information design using Inter: neutral, utilitarian, and instantly legible at small sizes on low-resolution screens under bright daylight.
