Button Visibility & Disabled State Explanations

Why hiding the button creates confusion, and how a disabled button that explains itself solves the problem.

13-button-hidden-version.png

The Rejected Pattern: Button Hidden

[Submit Button is Hidden Until Valid]

Why It Fails: Users expect to see the primary action. Hiding the button makes the page feel truncated or broken, leaving users wondering whether the page crashed.

14-button-disabled-version.png

The Shipped Pattern: Button Explains Itself

⚠️ Please enter your complete 4-digit security PIN to proceed.

Why It Wins: The button anchors the form visually. When clicked, it explains the exact missing requirement rather than silently refusing.
